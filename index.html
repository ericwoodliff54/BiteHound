🤖 Android (Chrome Browser)

If "Install app" is unavailable, tap Create shortcut (or Add to Home screen):

  1. In Chrome, tap the three dots (⋮) in the top right.

  3. Check "Open as window" and tap Add.
🍏 Apple iOS (Safari)
  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share icon (square with arrow ↑).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add in the top right.

Once added, RecallRadar runs in fullscreen mode without any browser URL bars.
