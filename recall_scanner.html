Food Safety & Legal Notice

Important consumer health & terms of use notice

1. Informational Convenience Tool Only

BiteHound is an independent consumer shopping aid designed to cross-reference public food safety data. This application does not provide medical, diagnostic, nutritional, or legal advice, and must never substitute for professional healthcare guidance or personal inspection.

2. Always Inspect Physical Packaging

Food manufacturers frequently alter recipes, production lines, and facility allergen protocols without advance notice. Cross-contamination may occur. You must always inspect the physical product package, printed ingredient list, lot codes, and allergen warnings before purchasing or consuming any food item.

3. "As-Is" Data & Limitation of Liability

Recall data and product ingredients are aggregated from third-party public resources including Open Food Facts, the FDA, and the USDA. Data is provided strictly "AS IS" and "AS AVAILABLE" with no warranties of completeness, accuracy, or real-time availability. To the fullest extent permitted by law, BiteHound and its creators assume no liability for allergic reactions, illness, injury, or damages arising from reliance on this application.

4. Non-Government Affiliation

BiteHound is an independent consumer tool. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, authorized by, or an official product of the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA), the U.S. Department of Agriculture (USDA), or any government body.

By continuing, you acknowledge that you will always verify printed labels on physical packaging before consumption.
